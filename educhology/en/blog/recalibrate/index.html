🔄 How do you stay motivated to recalibrate your career growth?

With a Master’s in Education and two decades in teaching, I could have been exempted from the #TTT (Train the Trainer) programme. Yet I chose not to.

Teaching in schools and training for adults from diverse professional backgrounds are different spaces. This felt like it was time for me to refresh, recalibrate, and re-evaluate myself.

I have to admit that I can be fussy when it comes to making a decision. Thank Selena K. and Iconic Training Solutions for the advice that helped me make the final call.

And then a master trainer, Azman Ithnin！

I respect Azman not only as a trainer, but also as a lifelong learner and a deeply grounded human being. His curiosity, integrity, and intentional way of living and learning were evident throughout the programme.

The feedback he gave to each trainee with such care during our presentations was heartwarming, constructive, precise, and incredibly observant.

Also, final wrap-up video he put together at the end of the 5 days have a way of staying with me long after the session ended.

The entire experience was eye-opening. I learned so much from every participant along the way.

Special thanks to Jonathan Chu, my buddy right beside me. His inspiration, initiative, kindness and enthusiasm truly brightened the room.

I’m grateful for the people I met and the conversations we shared. Looking back, this was one of the best decisions I’ve made in 2025.

After the certification, the accreditation process awaits as my next step in 2026.

⁉️ As your career evolves, what helps you stay motivated to recalibrate?
