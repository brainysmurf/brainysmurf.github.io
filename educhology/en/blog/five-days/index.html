Last week, I pushed through one of the most intense schedules I’ve had in a long time.

When I signed up for the TTT (Training the Trainer) programme, I thought, ‘5 days of classes will be fine!'

Well! It turned out to be more like a breathless marathon with full day sessions, preparation for the written test and presentation. I didn’t even get much sleep.

After a hectic 5 days, the weekend finally arrived.

I hoped for a quiet, relaxing break but I jumped straight into coaching and mentoring sessions as my DP (Diploma Programme) students geared up for their exams.

On top of that, my hubby returned from a business trip to Poland, which meant a mountain of household tasks waiting for me once again.

And as if that wasn’t enough, my to-do list for our upcoming trip just kept growing!

It really felt like the longest 8 days ever!

Now, I’m finally catching my breath here in hashtag#Langkawi. ☺️

As December gently winds down, I’m taking a moment to pause and reflect on the year.

Let’s wrap up the year with a sense of meaning and joy.

🙌 Wishing you all a moment to look back on your own small wins and personal milestones with celebration and gratitude!

💬 How are you closing out the year? What’s your end-of-year ritual to reset or recharge?
