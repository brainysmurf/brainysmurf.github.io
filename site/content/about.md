## About Me

Teacher-turned-product-leader. Still writing code. Still in EDU.

I'm Adam Morris, a Product Engineer who's spent a career at the intersection of technology and education. I started in the classroom, teaching IB courses and discovering firsthand what educators actually need from their tools. That experience led me into the open source world, building solutions for schools and teachers, and eventually into product management where I could shape tools at scale.

I believe the best education technology is built by people who've been in the classroom. I believe in open source, in building in public, and in the idea that software can genuinely improve how we teach and learn — if we build it with care.

This site is where I write about product thinking, software development, education technology, and the places where those things overlap.
